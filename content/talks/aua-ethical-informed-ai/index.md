In Season 2, Episode 21 of the American University of Armenia (AUA) Media Lab’s Focus Series, Professor Maria Baghramian, director of AUA’s Center for Ethics in Public Affairs, speaks with David Dao, co-founder and chief scientist of GainForest.Earth and Economies & Governance co-lead at Protocol Labs.

The conversation explores ethical and informed AI use in decision-making and policymaking, data sovereignty and Indigenous knowledge, AI’s environmental risks and benefits, and public trust.
