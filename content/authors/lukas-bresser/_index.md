Lukas develops strategies, tools, and programs to accelerate scientific progress and bring frontier technologies into use. He studies what helps or hinders progress in emerging fields and develops PL R&D's methodology for choosing, deploying, and evaluating interventions. His work includes field-progress measurement, AI-native research and coordination tools, and program development with researchers, funders, and partners across PL R&D's focus areas.

Lukas previously led operations at a decentralized cloud infrastructure company. Earlier, as a startup operator at Protocol Labs, he built and led the Builders Program. He began his career at the Boston Consulting Group in New York where he worked as a Project Leader, and holds master's degrees from Columbia University and Sciences Po, Paris in political science and economics.
